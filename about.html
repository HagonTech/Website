About

Built from the operator's side of the screen.

Hagon began with a simple frustration: powerful security tools can produce a great deal of data without producing enough clarity about what actually deserves attention.

Why Hagon

Security teams need understanding, not another pile of alerts.

Hagon is shaped by hands-on cybersecurity and systems-administration experience in environments where small teams carry broad responsibility.

The goal is to make external exposure understandable, traceable, and operational without hiding uncertainty, inventing conclusions, or requiring a specialist to translate every result. Hagon applies external attack surface management (EASM) visibility within an evidence-first continuous threat exposure management (CTEM) approach.

Built for

Lean IT and security teams

Approachable without reducing evidence quality or security judgment.

Understand before acting

Begin with what is already visible. Observation comes before judgment.

Evidence before conclusion

Preserve the distinction between suspicion, correlation, validation, and a finding.

Clarity over volume

Help teams understand what matters, why it matters, and what should happen next.

Stay through verification

Security work is not finished when an alert is created. Remediation should be checked and history should show the result.

Who it is for

Organizations with meaningful exposure and limited specialist bandwidth.

Hagon is initially focused on K-12 and local-government environments, small and midsize businesses, and professional organizations where broad IT responsibility often sits with lean teams.

The standard

Enterprise discipline without enterprise noise.

The product should remain understandable even as the underlying security judgment becomes more capable.

Know what is visible. Prove what matters. Verify what changed.
