About

Built from the operator’s side of the screen.

Hagon began with a simple frustration: powerful security tools often produce more data without producing enough clarity.

Why

Security teams need understanding, not another pile of alerts.

Hagon is being shaped by hands-on cybersecurity and systems-administration experience in environments where a small team carries broad responsibility.

The goal is to make external exposure understandable, traceable, and operational without hiding uncertainty or inventing conclusions.

Principles

Recon before scanning

Begin with what is already publicly visible.

Evidence before conclusions

Observations do not become Findings automatically.

Operations after discovery

Ownership, remediation, history, and Score belong in one accountable workflow.

Calm by design

The interface should reduce cognitive load instead of competing for attention.
