How It Works

Understand first.

Each stage has a clear responsibility. Evidence does not become risk simply because it was observed.

01

Recon

Observe authorized public targets and preserve canonical evidence.

02

Validation

Determine whether an observation supports a real, reproducible issue.

03

Findings

Create an operational record only when the evidence supports it.

04

Remediation

Assign ownership, track action, and preserve history.

05

Score

Reflect only validated Findings through explicit, explainable rules.

Observe without assuming. Act with evidence.
