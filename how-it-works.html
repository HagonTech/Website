How It Works

Understand first.

Each stage has a clear responsibility. Evidence does not become risk simply because it was observed.

Private beta

Access is coordinated directly.

Customer identity comes before durable organization state. Commercial access remains separate from technical authorization, whether beta participation is complimentary or paid.

01

Recon

See what the Internet already reveals about your organization and what a potential attacker can learn from it.

02

Verify & Authorize

Prove domain control, confirm public network scope, and explicitly choose which networks and exact hostnames Hagon is permitted to assess.

03

Active Recon

Safely observe authorized public networks and exact hostnames using bounded, non-destructive checks. Hostname authorization stays scoped to that logical web/TLS service and does not become general authority over a hosting provider’s IP.

04

Validation

Confirm which observed conditions actually represent security issues.

05

Findings

Turn validated evidence into clear, actionable Findings.

06

Remediation

Understand what matters, what to do, and what could be affected by the change.

07

Revalidation

Check the specific condition again. When trusted evidence shows the fix worked, the Finding resolves and Score reflects the recovery.

Score reflects what Hagon has validated. Observations and suspected issues do not lower your Score. Only validated Findings do. When remediation is verified, the Score reflects the change.

New exposure

Hagon maintains a baseline so newly observed services, hostnames, certificates, and other meaningful changes can be surfaced without turning every change into a Finding.

New vulnerabilities

Vulnerability Intelligence correlates newly disclosed issues against known technologies. Relevant authorized assets can be targeted for Validation instead of waiting for the next broad scan.

Nothing meaningful changed

Hagon stays quiet. Continuous monitoring should reduce noise, not manufacture engagement.

Observe without assuming. Act with evidence.
