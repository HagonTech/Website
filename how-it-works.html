How It Works

Understand first.

Each stage has a clear responsibility. Evidence does not become risk simply because it was observed.

01

Recon

See what the Internet already reveals about your organization.

02

Verify & Authorize

Prove control and choose which assets Hagon is permitted to assess.

03

Active Recon

Safely observe authorized systems directly using bounded, non-destructive checks.

04

Validation

Confirm which observed conditions actually represent security issues.

05

Findings

Turn validated evidence into clear, actionable Findings.

06

Remediation

Understand what matters, what to do, and what could be affected by the change.

07

Revalidation

Check the condition again and verify whether the fix worked.

Score reflects what Hagon has validated. Observations and suspected issues do not lower your Score. Only validated Findings do. When remediation is verified, the Score reflects the change.

Observe without assuming. Act with evidence.
