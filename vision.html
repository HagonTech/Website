Vision

Make exposure clear.

The Internet already knows things about your organization. hagon helps you see them, prove what matters, and act.

The problem

Security teams do not need more noise.

Most organizations already have scanners, alerts, and dashboards. What they often lack is a clear answer to four questions: What is exposed? Does it matter? Who owns it? What happens next?

Discovery is not understanding. More data does not always lead to better decisions.

The approach

Prove it before you call it a problem.

hagon starts with authorized Recon. Observations stay observations until Validation supports a Finding.

Recon Observe what is publicly visible.
Validation Determine what represents real exposure.
Findings Create evidence-backed operational records.
Remediation Assign ownership and track action.
Score Reflect only validated Findings.

The principles

Built for real operators.

01

Recon first

Start with what is already visible before deciding what requires active Validation.

02

Observations are not Findings

hagon keeps the boundary between evidence and conclusion explicit.

03

Every Score should be explainable

Score changes should trace directly to validated Findings and canonical rules.

04

Operations after discovery

Ownership, Remediation, history, and workflow are part of the product, not afterthoughts.

05

Calm by design

The interface should reduce cognitive load through restraint, hierarchy, and clear language.

06

Provider neutral

Scanners and intelligence sources contribute evidence. hagon owns the operational truth.

The audience

Built for lean security and IT teams.

hagon is for organizations with real cybersecurity responsibility and limited staff.

That includes school districts, municipalities, healthcare organizations, small and mid-sized businesses, and managed service providers supporting them.

The goal is simple. Make external exposure clear enough to act on.

Know what is visible. Validate what matters. Act with confidence.
