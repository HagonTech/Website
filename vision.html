Vision

Make exposure understandable.

hagon exists to help organizations understand what the Internet already knows about them, validate what matters, and turn real exposure into accountable action.

The problem

Security teams do not need more noise.

Most organizations already have scanners, alerts, dashboards, and vulnerability data. What they often lack is a clear, defensible way to understand what is truly exposed, why it matters, who owns it, and what should happen next.

Discovery is not the same as understanding. A larger list of assets, observations, or vulnerabilities does not automatically create better decisions.

The approach

Evidence before conclusions.

hagon begins with Recon: a customer-authorized view of what is already publicly visible. Those observations remain observations until Validation determines whether they support a real Finding.

Recon Observe what is publicly visible.
Validation Determine what represents real exposure.
Findings Create evidence-backed operational records.
Remediation Assign ownership and track action.
Score Reflect only validated Findings.

The principles

Designed for real operators.

01

Recon first

Start with what is already visible before deciding what requires active Validation.

02

Observations are not Findings

hagon keeps the boundary between evidence and conclusion explicit.

03

Every Score should be explainable

Score changes should trace directly to validated Findings and canonical rules.

04

Operations after discovery

Ownership, Remediation, history, and workflow are part of the product, not afterthoughts.

05

Calm by design

The interface should reduce cognitive load through restraint, hierarchy, and clear language.

06

Provider neutral

Scanners and intelligence sources contribute evidence. hagon owns the operational truth.

The audience

Built for lean security and IT teams.

hagon is being shaped for organizations that carry real cybersecurity responsibility without a large dedicated exposure-management staff.

That includes school districts, municipalities, healthcare organizations, small and mid-sized businesses, and managed service providers supporting them.

The goal is not to make enterprise security smaller. The goal is to make external exposure operationally clear.

Know what is visible. Validate what matters. Act with confidence.
