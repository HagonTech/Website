Vision

Make external exposure understandable.

Hagon is being built as a Recon-first Exposure Operations Platform for organizations that need continuous exposure management without a large security team.

The idea

Discovery is the beginning, not the product.

Attack-surface visibility answers an important question: what can the outside world see? Hagon begins there, then continues into authorization, direct observation, validation, prioritization, remediation, revalidation, and history.

In industry terms, Recon provides external attack-surface management capabilities while the complete operating loop aligns with Continuous Threat Exposure Management. The product itself should remain simpler than the acronyms.

For lean teams

Security expertise should not be a prerequisite for understanding the result.

School districts, municipalities, professional firms, nonprofits, SMBs, and midmarket organizations often carry meaningful exposure without dedicated exposure-management teams. Hagon should explain the evidence in plain language and keep the technical detail available underneath.

The operating loop

Know. Decide. Act. Verify. Remember.

Hagon should continuously determine what deserves attention, help the organization understand the required outcome, verify whether remediation worked, and preserve the history of how external posture changed.

Know what is visible. Validate what matters. Act with confidence.
