Roadmap

Build the operating path in order.

The roadmap records capability direction, not delivery dates. Hagon should earn additional authority only as the controls needed to use it safely are established.

Available now

Recon foundation

Passive DNS, email-security and TLS observations, reputation-provider boundaries, exposure correlation, baselines, change explanation, dashboard experience, and runtime integration.

Next

Domain control & Asset Authorization

DNS control verification and durable customer authority for the specific infrastructure Hagon may actively assess.

Then

Active Recon & Validation

Bounded service discovery, technology identification, TLS and HTTP inspection, and targeted safe validators that produce evidence before Findings.

Following

Attention & Revalidation

Prioritize what deserves action, guide remediation, and verify whether the specific condition is actually gone.

Continuous

Vulnerability Intelligence

Correlate newly disclosed vulnerabilities against known customer technologies and target relevant authorized assets for rapid Validation.

Later

Integrations & expanded operations

Ticketing, SIEM, APIs, customer administration, enterprise identity, private scanning, and other capabilities that extend the operating model without changing its boundaries.

Capability follows authority. Evidence follows observation.
