Privacy

Collect only what is needed.

This website is currently an informational private-beta site. It does not provide public account registration or a public contact form.

Website data

The static site does not intentionally request personal information from visitors.

Product data

The Hagon application preserves tenant-scoped Recon observations and operational records needed to provide the private-beta service. Direct interaction with customer infrastructure requires explicit authorization. Hagon may also collect bounded public website intelligence, such as business contact information, login surfaces, technology clues, provider relationships, and other information already exposed on the public Internet, when needed to explain organizational exposure.

Evidence, not collection

Hagon retains durable normalized security knowledge while minimizing short-lived raw collection artifacts and unnecessary sensitive content. Public information is collected to explain organizational exposure, not to build personal profiles or harvest credentials.

AI use

Customer-specific security data is not intended for training public or general-purpose AI models. Any future external AI processing will require data minimization and accurate disclosure.

Tracking

No advertising or behavioral-tracking claims are made by this notice. Hosting infrastructure may retain ordinary request and security logs.

Changes

This notice will be expanded before public registration, contact intake, analytics, or commercial service is introduced.
