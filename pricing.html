Discovery is included

Start with your primary domain. Hagon can discover additional internet-facing infrastructure without turning every observation into a billable asset.

02

You authorize the scope

Commercial scope is based on public assets you explicitly bring into authorized coverage. Discovery alone does not expand your paid scope or grant Hagon permission to interact with an asset.

03

Findings do not change the price

Whether Hagon validates one issue or twenty, the price stays tied to your authorized asset tier. There is no per-finding incentive.

04

Monitoring is part of the lifecycle

Validation, findings, remediation guidance, revalidation, continuous monitoring, security activity, and customer notifications are part of the Hagon experience at every tier.
