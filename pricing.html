Pricing

Protect the scope. Do not meter the problems.

Pricing is being designed around the external environment a customer protects, not the number of assets, observations, or Findings Hagon discovers.

01

Protected scope

Early plans are expected to begin with a verified domain and a bounded set of public IP addresses.

02

No discovery penalty

Finding more subdomains, services, certificates, or issues should increase delivered value, not unexpectedly increase the invoice.

03

Early access

Commercial pricing is not yet published. Early design partners will help validate packaging before public launch.

Pricing is not final.

Hagon is currently in private alpha. No public plan names or prices have been announced.
