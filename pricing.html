Pricing

Price the scope. Not the problems.

Hagon pricing is being designed around the public IP space a customer explicitly authorizes for active assessment. Discovering more should never feel like a financial penalty.

01

First domain included

Start Recon with the organization’s primary domain. Discovered subdomains do not increase the bill.

02

Authorized IP scope

The primary capacity measure is the public address space the customer authorizes Hagon to actively assess, not the number of addresses that happen to respond.

03

Unlimited discovery

New hostnames, services, certificates, technologies, third-party relationships, and unknown assets discovered through Recon do not automatically change pricing.

04

No surprise overages

If adding newly identified infrastructure would cross a capacity boundary, Hagon should show that before the customer expands authorized scope.

Same Hagon.

Smaller customers should receive the same quality of Recon, Vulnerability Intelligence, Validation, evidence, remediation guidance, and revalidation as larger customers.

No per-Finding charges.

Hagon should never make money by finding more problems. Scans, Findings, remediation, and revalidation are part of the operating lifecycle.

Current direction.

Early capacity bands are being evaluated around small, medium, and /24-sized authorized public footprints. Final plan limits and prices have not been announced.

Pricing is not final. Hagon is currently in private alpha. Commercial plans will be published only after the operating model has been validated with real environments.
