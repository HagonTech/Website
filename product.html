Product

See it. Prove it. Act.

Hagon combines external attack-surface visibility with continuous threat exposure operations. Discovery starts the process. Evidence, action, and verification finish it.

Recon

See what is already visible.

Passive Recon begins with the organization’s domains and public footprint. Hagon collects and correlates public DNS, certificate, email-security, reputation, Internet-exposure, technology, and related observations without directly interacting with customer infrastructure.

New hostnames, services, providers, website exposure, and relationships become part of a durable external baseline. Hagon interprets that knowledge in plain language so customers can understand what exists, what changed, and what a potential attacker can learn.

Recon produces

Observations

Discovery is evidence, not a conclusion.

Authorization

Customer control comes before active testing.

Domain verification establishes DNS control. Asset Authorization is separate. Customers choose which public infrastructure Hagon may actively assess and can revoke or pause that authority.

Discovered third-party or unknown infrastructure remains visible without being automatically authorized.

Boundary

Explicit authority

Discovery never grants permission to scan.

Validation

Do not confuse visibility with proof.

Authorized Active Recon can observe services and technologies directly. Validation then determines whether the accumulated evidence establishes a real security condition.

A banner, version match, or newly published CVE may create a candidate exposure. It does not automatically create a Finding.

Possible result

Confirmed. Not confirmed. Inconclusive.

Hagon preserves uncertainty instead of inventing certainty.

Operations

Turn validated exposure into accountable work.

Findings carry evidence, severity, Action Priority, ownership, remediation guidance, lifecycle, and history. Hagon explains what is wrong, why it matters, what to do, what could go wrong during the change, and how the fix will be verified.

The question

What should I do next?

Attention is reserved for work that deserves human action.

Continuous operations

Verify the fix. Watch what changes.

After remediation, Hagon revalidates the specific condition. Score changes only when evidence changes. Vulnerability Intelligence can correlate newly disclosed vulnerabilities against technologies Hagon already knows about and target relevant authorized systems for Validation.

Customer view

Summary

A concise operational digest brings together current exposure, validated Findings, meaningful changes, adversary context, and the next action without making the customer analyze raw scanner output.

Vulnerability intelligence

Connect new vulnerabilities to what is actually exposed.

Hagon continuously ingests public vulnerability intelligence and correlates it with technologies observed in the customer’s external footprint. Relevant matches remain candidate exposures until Hagon can validate them with sufficient evidence.

Long-term loop

Know. Decide. Act. Verify. Remember.

History turns isolated scans into an operational record.

Discovery starts the conversation. Operations finish it.
