See the outside first.

Hagon begins with passive Recon to build an outside-in view from domains, public infrastructure, certificates, email security, technologies, provider relationships, and other observable signals.

Prove before concluding.

Observations and vulnerability matches are not Findings. Hagon separates discovery from Validation so suspected exposure does not become risk simply because a scanner or feed reported it.

Understand the attacker’s view.

Hagon turns external observations into plain-language context that explains what a potential attacker can already learn. When a condition can be proven, it moves into Evidence, a Finding, remediation, revalidation, and history.

Stay with the problem.

Hagon stays with the problem after discovery. Remediation can be reported, the condition can be revalidated, and Score changes only when validated exposure changes.
