See the outside first.

Hagon begins with passive Recon to build an external view from domains, public infrastructure, certificates, email security, technologies, and other observable signals.

Prove before concluding.

Observations and vulnerability matches are not Findings. Hagon separates discovery from Validation so suspected exposure does not become risk simply because a scanner or feed reported it.

Stay with the problem.

Validated exposure moves into priority, ownership, remediation, revalidation, and history. Hagon is built for continuous exposure operations, not another point-in-time report.
